Welcome to Flow Chart Builder

Build curved, ribbon-style flow diagrams that are hard to make in PowerPoint, Excel or Publisher.

  1. Arc: click the center, then the start point, then the end point to draw a thick curved band.
  2. Bézier: click a start and an end point, then drag the two filled control points to make S-shapes and swoops.
  3. Arrow: click on a band, or near its end, to add a chevron that follows the curve.
  4. Text and Circle: add free labels and numbered badges such as ①.
  5. Icon: put an icon with a label inside a loop, such as a cloud with "Data". Title: add a heading and subtitle.
  6. Click any shape, then use the panel on the right to change its color, text and size.
  7. Save JSON downloads your diagram. Load JSON opens it again later.
  8. Snap it: closes the gaps between your bands and joins them into one figure in a single color. Then click on it with Arrow wherever you want arrows. Each part between arrows becomes its own piece, with its own color and text.
  9. Trace image: upload a picture of a flowchart to show it behind the canvas and draw over it. Clear image removes it when you're done.

Band ends snap together so pieces join cleanly. Click Example to see a finished diagram.
